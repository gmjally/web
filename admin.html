Export & Update Instructions

Option 1 — Edit here, then ask Claude to update

  1. Make your edits in any section on the left
  2. Click Export JSON (top right) to download your updated content file
  3. Open a conversation with Claude and say:
    "Here is my updated content.json — please update the three website pages"
  4. Paste or attach the JSON file — Claude will rebuild and republish all three pages

Option 2 — Edit here, copy JSON, tell Claude to update

  1. Make your edits in any section on the left
  2. Click Show JSON below to see the current content
  3. Copy the entire JSON
  4. Paste it to Claude with the instruction above

Quick edits — tell Claude directly

For simple additions you can just tell Claude without the JSON:
"Add a new grant: title X, authors Y, funder Z, amount $W, year 2025"
"Add this journal paper to the publications list: ..."
"Add [name] to current PhD students"
